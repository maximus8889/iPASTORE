How do updates work?

The download link points to the latest published GitHub release. An app update still needs valid signing and your approval in iOS. Refreshing a signature and updating the app are separate steps.

Why does a Home Screen icon open iPAStore?

Apps installed inside iPAStore run in its container. Their icons are shortcuts. To make a separate iPhone app, select On iPhone under Settings → Installation, add a matching certificate and profile, and install the app again.

What powers free setup?

The external setup tool is SideInstaller by FrizzleM. iPAStore uses the LiveContainer runtime and a bundled SideStore signing engine. You may still need to import pairing and connect your account inside iPAStore after a custom IPA install.
